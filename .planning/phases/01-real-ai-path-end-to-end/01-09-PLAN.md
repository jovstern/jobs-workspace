## Flagged assumptions

- **Costs are estimates.** RESEARCH.md assumes about two US cents per fit check with advice; this spike requests ratings only, so each call should cost less. The 30-request cap bounds the spend whatever the real price is.
- **Stability rule.** The rule (all six required met for the near-perfect pair, at most two for the poor pair, at most one flip per pair on average) is this plan's choice under "Claude's Discretion". If the user wants a stricter or looser bar, say so at the Task 2 stop, before Task 3 runs.
- **Spike items not in this phase.** S3 and S4 (profile and PDF models) belong to Phase 01.2; S6 and S8 (quote pass rate on the golden set, injection fixture) to Phase 01.1, though this run already records rejected-quote counts; S5 (bad-key shape) is optional and not run; S9 is the billing decision in plan 01-11.
