  <decision>Does the Google project behind GEMINI_API_KEY have an active billing account (paid tier)?</decision>
  <context>
    The approved privacy note has a paragraph that says: "On Google's paid tier, Google says it does not use this content to improve its products. It keeps logs of requests for a limited time, only to detect abuse and meet legal obligations." Google's terms (effective 23 March 2026, RESEARCH A.6) make that true only when the API is used through a Cloud project with an active billing account. On the free tier Google may use content to improve its products and human reviewers may read it. Research could not check the project's billing status without a live call, and neither can an agent: it is visible only in Google AI Studio. D-02 records that the key is on the paid tier; this checkpoint is the confirmation before the sentence is shown to a user. The UI-SPEC rule is: if this cannot be confirmed, delete that paragraph and nothing else.
