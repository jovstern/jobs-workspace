<objective>
Turn the ratings into the result the user came for: a score and band computed in code (D-10,
D-11), and a result panel that shows every requirement with its rating, its resume quote and a
short reason, plus honest states for "nothing yet", "running" and "failed".

Purpose: FIT-03 and the display half of FIT-02, and the proof of FIT-08 and DATA-01 for analyses:
a stored result is shown instantly and never triggers another AI call.

Output: the shared score function, pure selectors, the finished fit panel with its two section
components, the `Badge` component, safe mutation defaults, and the full main-flow browser test.

Formula (Claude's discretion within D-10): required requirements carry the score. Met is full
credit, partial is half, not evidenced is none, averaged over the required group. Nice-to-have
items, averaged the same way, add at most 10 percent on top and cannot lift a job with no
required credit above 0. Bands: 80 and above Strong fit, 60 and above Good fit, 40 and above
Stretch, below 40 Long shot. All required met with no nice-to-have met gives 90.
</objective>
