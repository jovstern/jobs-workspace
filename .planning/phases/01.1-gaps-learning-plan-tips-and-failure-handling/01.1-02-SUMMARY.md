## Accomplishments
- `buildFitView` now returns `gaps` (required and nice-to-have, posting order, first plan per requirement, plans for met requirements ignored), `hasAdvice` and `tailoring`. The score is unchanged with or without advice.
- `GapsSection`, `GapItem` and `LearningPlanBlock` render the section under Requirements; it is keyed on `analysedAt` so open plans reset on a new result.
- `AdviceMissingNotice` gives an old result a Check again that runs the same `startCheck` as the header button. Focus handling no longer uses state or a mount callback.
- E2E fit requests sent by a full `e2e/check-fit.spec.ts` run: 8 (main flow 1, bands 2, rejected quote 1, retry 2, old-result upgrade 2), under the limit of 10.
- Stand-in counts confirmed in the browser: Senior Data Scientist has 6 required gaps (4 not evidenced, 2 partial) and 2 nice-to-have; Senior Frontend Engineer has 0 required and 1 nice-to-have. The hallucinated_quote scenario leaves exactly one gap with no plan.
