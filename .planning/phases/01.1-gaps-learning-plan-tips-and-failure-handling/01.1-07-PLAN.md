## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** see what is missing and what to learn next, and to recover cleanly when the AI or my saved data fails, **so that** every fit check leaves me with a next step.

<objective>
Optional, and only with the user's go-ahead at that moment: check the two things about this phase
that no stand-in test can prove. (1) One smoke call: does Google accept the new nested answer
schema, and does a real answer parse? If it did not, every real fit check would fail while every
automated test stays green. (2) A short measurement: how long does the larger answer take, and do
the output cap, the deadline and the "Usually 10 to 20 seconds." line fit what is measured?

Purpose: research Open Question 1 and the "Claude's Discretion" items on model settings. Every
other plan in this phase is complete and verified without this one. If the user declines, the
defaults chosen in plan 01.1-01 stand and that is recorded as an open, unverified item.

Output: two new modes in the existing spike script, each rehearsable against the stand-in at no
cost; a results file that records the decision; and, only if the measurement ran, model settings
and the reassurance line set from the numbers.

This plan runs last and is the only one in the phase that can call the real model. It reads no
resume but `e2e/fixtures/resumes/frontend-senior.txt`.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

- **Costs are estimates** from the intro prices recorded in Phase 1 (research A15). The request
  caps (1 and 10) bound the spend whatever the real price is.
- **One smoke sample is not a latency measurement.** A slow or truncated smoke call raises a
  setting in the safe direction and says so; only the measurement supports a typical figure.
- **Measured values may raise the defaults and never lower them.** A cap higher than needed costs
  nothing unless the model rambles; a cap or deadline that is too low throws away a paid answer.
- **The reassurance sentence is rebuilt from the same template** ("Usually {a} to {b} seconds.")
  if the measurement puts typical time outside 10 to 20 seconds. This is the one-string change the
  UI contract allows; the user sees it described at the Task 2 stop.
- **A rejected schema is not fixed in this plan.** It is reported as a blocking finding, because
  changing the schema shown to Google needs a design decision and another paid confirmation.
- **Names of courses or providers in real plans, and suggested rewrites in real tips, are not
  checked here.** They are the manual rows in `01.1-VALIDATION.md` and need a person to read real
