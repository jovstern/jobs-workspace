## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** see what is missing and what to learn next, and to recover cleanly when the AI or my saved data fails, **so that** every fit check leaves me with a next step.

<objective>
Make the advice half of the fit answer honest. After this plan every tailoring tip quotes text
that really is in the resume, every keyword really is in the posting and really is absent from
the resume, and no plan step, tip or keyword carries a link.

Purpose: FIT-07 ("never suggest claiming something the resume does not support") and the code
half of D-E04. Plan 01.1-01 returned `tailoring: null` on purpose; this plan turns tailoring on
together with all of its checks, so no unverified tip is ever stored.

Output: `advice-text.ts` (links, platform names, keyword filter), `occursAsPhrase` exported from
`quotes.ts`, the full `buildAdvice`, the stand-in scenario `advice_partial`, and a route-level
test file for advice.

What code cannot enforce, stated plainly: that a plan names no course, book or product (only
links and a short list of platform names are detectable), and that tip advice proposes no new
resume wording. Both are prompt rules from plan 01.1-01 and manual checks in `01.1-VALIDATION.md`.
</objective>

  tests assert properties (no `example.com`, ratings equal, quotes verified) rather than exact
