## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** see what is missing and what to learn next, and to recover cleanly when the AI or my saved data fails, **so that** every fit check leaves me with a next step.

<objective>
Tracer, browser half. The learning plans that plan 01.1-01 put in the fit answer now reach the
user: the result panel gains a Gaps section, split into required and nice-to-have, each gap with
its plan, and a result saved in Phase 1 gets a notice that offers to add them.

Purpose: FIT-04 and the visible half of FIT-06. Gaps are derived in render from the stored
ratings (no AI, nothing new stored), which is also what makes old results work (D-E08).

Output: `buildFitView` extended with gaps, plans and tailoring; `GapsSection`, `GapItem`,
`LearningPlanBlock`, `AdviceMissingNotice`; the panel wired; browser tests that show a required
gap with its plan, a collapsed nice-to-have plan, and the old-result notice.

Styling stays plain. Copy is verbatim from `01.1-UI-SPEC.md`. The Tailoring section is plan
01.1-06; per-code error messages and staged progress are plan 01.1-08.
</objective>
