## Phase Goal

**As a** job seeker, **I want to** see what is missing and what to learn next, and to recover cleanly when the AI or my saved data fails, **so that** every fit check leaves me with a next step.

<objective>
Show the tailoring tips. Plan 01.1-03 made the server return tips whose quotes are verified
against the resume and keywords that are in the posting and absent from the resume; plan 01.1-02
put them in the fit view. This plan renders them, with the fixed sentences that keep the advice
honest.

Purpose: the visible half of FIT-07. The honesty of the content is enforced on the server; the
page's part is to show a quote with every tip, to show the fixed intro and the fixed note, and to
render model text as text only.

Output: `TailoringSection`, the read-only `Chip`, the panel wired, component tests for every
state in the UI contract, and browser assertions on a real check.

Styling stays plain. Copy is verbatim from `01.1-UI-SPEC.md` "Tailoring".
</objective>
