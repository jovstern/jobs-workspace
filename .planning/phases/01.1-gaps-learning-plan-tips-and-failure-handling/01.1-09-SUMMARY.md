**Eighteen error codes with one shared table of statuses, retry flags and fixed sentences, and a pure rule that maps a failure status and category to exactly one code.**

## Accomplishments
- Nine new `ai_*` codes in `shared/src/error.ts` with `ERROR_MESSAGES` typed over every code, so a missing sentence fails the type check; the server's `ERROR_TABLE` carries the status and flag for all eighteen and `AppError` reads its message from the shared table (the two local sentences are gone).
- The refused page address and the app's own limiter now answer over HTTP with their own sentences; the limiter case is pinned with a limit of 1 (second request 429, `Retry-After` digits, one model call).
- `failureToErrorCode` (TDD: 26 cases) turns a closed diagnostic into one code; timeout and blocked beat any status. It has no caller until plan 01.1-05.
