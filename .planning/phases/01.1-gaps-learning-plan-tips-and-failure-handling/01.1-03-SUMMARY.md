**Server-side verification of model advice: link-free learning plans, resume-quoted tailoring tips and posting-sourced keywords, each failing item dropped whole without ever failing the analysis.**

## Accomplishments
- Plans: link and learning-platform text in a step or why-text is rejected, a bad step costs only that step, steps are capped at four, and the first surviving plan per gap wins. Plans for met or unknown requirements are dropped.
- Tips: each quote must be a string of at most 600 characters that passes the existing `verifyQuote`, with usable advice; duplicates are removed on the normalised quote and the list is capped at five.
- Keywords: 2 to 40 characters, at most four words, no link, present in the posting and absent from the resume under case, slash, `.js`, plural and hyphenation folding; deduplicated and capped at eight.
- New `advice_partial` stand-in scenario plus a route test through the real SDK showing identical ratings, HTTP 200 and a single upstream call.

## Decisions Made
- One surviving step keeps the plan; an over-long step list is cut to four. This matches the plan's rule that one bad step never costs a plan.
- Link regex tightened (`data:` requires a media type and slash) so ordinary prose is not flagged.
- Platform deny-list kept as a best-effort check; it is documented in code as not exhaustive.
