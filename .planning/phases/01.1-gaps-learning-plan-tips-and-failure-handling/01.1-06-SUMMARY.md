# Phase 01.1 Plan 06: Tailoring tips Summary

**A fit check now ends with a "Tailoring your resume for this job" section: the fixed honesty intro, each tip as "From your resume:" plus the verified quote plus the advice, and the posting's missing words as read-only chips under "Add only if true for you.", with every empty and unavailable state worded and model text rendered as text only.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 10 min
- **Tasks:** 2 (1 tracer, 1 TDD-marked)
- **Files modified:** 6 (3 created, 3 modified)

## Accomplishments

- `Chip` is a plain `span` with the border, padding and `break-words` classes; no role, no handler, no tab stop. A comment says the remove button and other variants arrive in Phase 01.2.
- `TailoringSection` takes `Tailoring | null`. `null` gives the heading and the unavailable sentence only. Otherwise: intro, the "Emphasise what you already have" list (or its empty line), the keywords sub-heading, the note only when at least one chip exists, and the chip list (or "No missing keywords found."). Nothing is capped, sorted, filtered or matched in the browser.
- `FitPanel` renders it in a `mt-12` wrapper after the Gaps wrapper when `view.hasAdvice` is true; for a result with no advice nothing renders, and the existing old-result notice covers the tips.
- Browser test: the stand-in "strong match and a poor match" test now also asserts, after a real Senior Data Scientist check, the heading, the intro, at least one tip item containing "From your resume:", the note, and at least four chips. The stand-in returned enough on its own, so the counts in the plan held and nothing had to be re-pinned. No fit request was added to the file.
